About Messi

The Little Magician 🇦🇷

Born in Rosario, Argentina, Lionel Messi developed into one of football's most recognizable players. His left foot, close control, passing, finishing and ability to create chances have defined his playing style.

Messi spent the majority of his European career with FC Barcelona before moving to Paris Saint-Germain and later Inter Miami. With Argentina, he won the 2021 Copa América, 2022 FIFA World Cup and 2024 Copa América.
